## ✨ Key Features

### 🎛️ 1. Motion Tools & Comp Controls
* **Power Timeline controls:** Fully automated **Precomp**, **Crop Comp**, and **Add to Render Queue (RQ)** utilities.
* **Smart Trim bounds:** The Precomp and Null generators automatically scan selected layer boundaries in the timeline to dynamically align `inPoint` and `outPoint` bounds perfectly, preventing timeline clutter.

### 📐 2. Quick Layers & Anchor Point Engine
* **One-Click Quick Layers:** Instantly spawn Solid, Adjustment, Text, Shape, Camera, or Light layers.
* **Instant 3x3 Grid Snapping:** Snaps the anchor point to any corner or center of selected layers with dynamic positional compensation to prevent layers from shifting visually.

### 📈 3. Kinetics & Custom Ease Library
* **Keyframe Wingman:** Premium bezier slider graph interface to visually adjust keyframe velocities and influences dynamically.
* **My Ease Library:** Save, catalog, rename, and quickly apply custom custom-made easing presets.
* **Wiggle Pro:** Professional, loopable mathematical wiggle generator with real-time waveform visualizer previews.

### 🎨 4. Aesthetic & High-Contrast Design System
* **Cohesive Premium Colors:** Harmony-tailored, card-specific icon coloring rules (Indigo, Royal Blue, Violet, Amber, Orange, Rose) that avoid any generic browser defaults or green/teal (toska) visual clutter.
* **Smart Panel Mode:** Keeps track of your Top 5 most clicked utility buttons and adds a flat, clean `2px` stroke border outline to make them pop elegantly.
